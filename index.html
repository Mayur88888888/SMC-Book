Welcome

This book is a detailed engineering treatment of the thermoset SMC compression molding process, spanning materials, flow, cure, heat transfer, compaction, tooling/press design, defects, quality control, and simulation.

How equations are shown

For now, equations are written in LaTeX/plain-text form inside code blocks (not rendered). You can later enable MathJax/KaTeX with minimal edits.

How this is sourced

Core claims about the impact of cure cycle and process parameters on flow, degree of cure, thickness/consolidation, porosity, surface finish, and demolding risk are anchored to the provided handbook excerpts.
