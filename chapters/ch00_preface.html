Preface, scope, and how to use this book

Scope

This book is a deep engineering reference on thermoset SMC compression molding. It covers: constituent roles and maturation, press cycle design, flow and charge placement, viscosity evolution during cure, cure kinetics and heat transfer, compaction and porosity, tooling and press design, defects and troubleshooting, process control, testing, and simulation.

Important: process sensitivity

Many outcomes depend on the thermal history and pressure/flow history: the state of cure drives properties such as glass transition temperature, viscosity, cure shrinkage, stiffness, and thermal expansion. Processing parameters (temperature, pressure, time, vacuum, viscosity/flow) directly influence porosity and surface finish, and demolding/tool cleaning can damage surface and integrity. These themes are explicitly supported by the provided handbook excerpts.

How equations and derivations are presented

You requested “everything” but no equation rendering yet. Therefore:

  • Equations are written in LaTeX/plain-text inside <pre> blocks.
  • Each derivation is paired with the engineering meaning and how to measure inputs.
  • When you want, we can enable MathJax with one script line (no content rewrite required).

Notation (core variables)

  • T: temperature (K or °C as stated)
  • t: time
  • p: pressure (mold/press as defined)
  • η: viscosity (apparent/process-relevant)
  • α: degree of cure (0 to 1)
  • Tg: glass transition temperature
  • Hg, Hr: reaction enthalpy / heat of reaction terms

How to read (recommended path)

  1. If you are new: Ch01 → Ch02 → Ch08 → Ch13 (fundamentals, workflow, porosity, defects).
  2. If you are debugging production: Ch10 → Ch11 → Ch13 (press/cycle, control, defects).
  3. If you are doing process modeling: Ch04 → Ch06 → Ch16 (rheology, heat/cure, simulation).
